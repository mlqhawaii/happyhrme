Ala Moana Happy Hour Guide

Ala Moana is one of Honolulu’s easiest areas for happy hour because restaurants, hotel bars and shopping-center venues are concentrated within a relatively small area between Waikīkī and Kakaʻako. That makes it practical to compare an early drink, a food-heavy happy hour or a stop before an evening in town.

Choosing an Ala Moana happy hour

Ala Moana deals vary widely: some emphasize discounted beer and cocktails, while others are more useful for small plates or an early dinner. Because the neighborhood sits between several major visitor and residential areas, timing and parking can matter as much as the headline price.

  • Compare food value as well as drink prices.
  • Check whether the deal is inside Ala Moana Center, a hotel, or a nearby standalone venue.
  • Use the live map when walking between Ala Moana and Kakaʻako.
  • Confirm the current happy-hour window before making a special trip.
