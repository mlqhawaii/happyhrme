Hawaiʻi Island Happy Hour Guide

Hawaiʻi Island is too large for a single undifferentiated happy-hour list. Kona and Waikōloa serve West Hawaiʻi, while Hilo covers the east side; driving between those regions can take hours. HappyHr.Me therefore treats location as a core part of the search rather than an afterthought.

Happy hour by side of Hawaiʻi Island

Kailua-Kona has a concentration of waterfront and Aliʻi Drive venues, Waikōloa adds resort-area options farther north, and Hilo has its own local restaurants and bars. The live map lets you stay within the side of the island you are actually visiting.

  • Kona: waterfront restaurants, beer, Mai Tais and pupu specials.
  • Waikōloa / Kohala: resort-area choices on the west side.
  • Hilo: local east-side restaurants and bars.
  • Do not choose purely by price—distance matters more here than on Oʻahu.
