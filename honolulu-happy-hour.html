HAWAIʻI HAPPY HOUR GUIDE

Best Happy Hours in Honolulu

Honolulu happy hour is spread across several very different districts. Waikīkī tends to have the densest concentration of visitor-friendly deals, Ala Moana is convenient for shopping and after-work drinks, Kakaʻako has breweries and newer restaurants, and Kaimukī/Kāhala adds neighborhood options east of downtown.

How to use the Honolulu happy hour map

Start with the neighborhood you will actually be in, then compare hours and deal details. A five-dollar beer in Ala Moana may be less useful if you are already walking in Waikīkī, while an early Kakaʻako happy hour can be a better fit before dinner. The live finder keeps these areas separate and shows source links so you can confirm a deal before going.

  • Use the area filter first; Honolulu traffic makes distance matter.
  • Check whether the deal is drinks only or also includes food and pupus.
  • Look at the published source when timing is important.
  • Use Open now when deciding where to go immediately.
