Kīhei Happy Hour Guide

Kīhei is one of Maui’s most practical places for happy hour because restaurants and bars are spread along a relatively short stretch of South Kīhei Road and nearby shopping centers. Current listings include casual food, sushi, beer and cocktail specials, making it easier to find something that fits a beach-day or early-dinner schedule.

Choosing a Kīhei happy hour

Kīhei deals are useful when you compare more than the headline price. Some venues emphasize discounted food, others drinks, and some have longer windows that work better after a late beach session. HappyHr.Me keeps the Kīhei listings in one area and links to the source used to verify each deal.

  • Compare the happy-hour window with your actual South Maui plans.
  • Look for food-specific deals if happy hour is replacing dinner.
  • A venue with a slightly higher drink price may offer much better food value.
  • Use the live map to avoid driving north or south unnecessarily.
