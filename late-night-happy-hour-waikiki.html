Late-Night Happy Hour Waikīkī Guide

Waikīkī has more late-evening dining and drinking options than most parts of Hawaiʻi, but true late-night happy hours are less common than early-afternoon deals. The key is distinguishing normal bar hours from an actual discounted late-night menu.

Finding a late-night Waikīkī deal

Use the live finder’s time controls and source links to confirm whether a venue really offers a second happy hour or simply stays open late. Walking distance can matter more than a small price difference after dark.

  • Confirm that the discount is actually offered late at night.
  • Check food-kitchen closing times separately from bar hours.
  • Prioritize walkability when staying in Waikīkī.
