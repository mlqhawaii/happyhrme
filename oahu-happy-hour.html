HAWAIʻI HAPPY HOUR GUIDE

Oʻahu Happy Hour Guide

Oʻahu has the broadest mix of happy-hour neighborhoods in Hawaiʻi, from resort-heavy Waikīkī and the restaurant clusters of Ala Moana and Kakaʻako to Kailua, Ko Olina and Haleʻiwa. The useful question is rarely just who has happy hour—it is which deal is close to where you already are, what time it runs, and whether the published menu is still current.

Where to look for happy hour on Oʻahu

Waikīkī is dense and walkable, Kakaʻako and Ala Moana work well for after-work drinks, and the island’s windward, west-side and North Shore venues make more sense when you can filter geographically. HappyHr.Me groups the live listings by area so you can compare nearby options instead of sorting through restaurants across the entire island.

  • Waikīkī: hotel bars, restaurants, late-afternoon and evening specials.
  • Kakaʻako / Ala Moana: strong after-work clusters with food and cocktail deals.
  • Kailua / Kāneʻohe: windward options without driving back into town.
  • Ko Olina / Kapolei and Haleʻiwa: useful resort and North Shore choices.
