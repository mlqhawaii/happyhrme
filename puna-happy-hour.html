Puna Happy Hour Guide

Puna has fewer happy-hour venues than Hilo or Kona, but neighborhood options can be useful for residents and travelers staying on the southeast side of Hawaiʻi Island.

Finding happy hour in Puna

Because the area is spread out and venues may keep shorter evening schedules, it is important to verify current hours before driving. Hilo can provide more options if you are already heading north.

  • Check distance and closing time.
  • Compare Hilo when practical.
